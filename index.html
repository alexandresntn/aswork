About

Alexandre Santana & Co

"The challenge is to deeply understand the core of each business and apply those findings to an authentic, functional brand identity. From there, the work expands across every layer of the commercial experience, from positioning to communication and digital touchpoints."

The focus of every project is to align the company's commercial strategy with its brand strategy to reach its goals. Creating experiences of value for people and competitive advantages in the market.
